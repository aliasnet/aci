Luck Engineering Protocol 

Overview

Source: Thinking and Destiny — Harold Waldwin Percival (often misattributed as “Perl”)
Year: 1946
Length: ~1080 pages

Core Thesis

Life is not random. It is the externalization of thought over time.

Thought is treated as a causal unit that generates structured outcomes across physical, psychological, and mental domains. Reality is framed as a balancing system rather than a random sequence of events.


---

Triune Self Model

The Doer

Seat of desire and lived experience

Operates through emotional and sensory engagement

Often mistaken as the whole self


The Thinker

Seat of reasoning and structure

Organizes impressions and directs attention

Less embedded in sensory identity


The Knower

Direct apprehension of truth or “what is”

Intuitive and non-discursive

Stable reference point when accessible



---

Mind Structure Model

Body Mind

Driven by sensory input and reaction

Prefers stimulation and narrative conflict


Feeling Mind

Gives emotional shape to perception

Stabilizes or distorts experience depending on alignment


Desire Mind

Generates propulsion toward outcomes

Can override reasoning when unbalanced


Structural Note

In most individuals, desire overrides reason, producing misaligned decision cycles.


---

Law of Thought

Core Principle

A thought becomes a causal entity with three domains of expression:

Physical outcomes

Psychological states

Mental conditioning


Operational Mechanism

Thoughts persist until balanced through experience:

Expression

Resistance

Resolution

Consequence


Key Constraint

No thought is neutral once stabilized. Each generates future conditions that resolve imbalance.


---

Four Stages of Thinking

1. Select — choose a single subject of focus


2. Hold — maintain attention without fragmentation


3. Compress — reduce to essential structure


4. Focus — allow insight without forced conclusion




---

Four Destiny Bands

Physical Destiny

Psychic Destiny

Emotional and atmospheric states

Mental Destiny

Patterns of thought and interpretation

Noetic Destiny

Direct awareness or intuitive knowing

Three Core Tools

Pause

Interrupt automatic reaction loops

Revoke

---

Practical Protocols

Regenerative Breathing

Upright posture

Soft gaze

Nose inhalation, longer exhalation

10–15 minutes daily

No forced retention


Weekly Balance

Resolve one unresolved cognitive or emotional imbalance:

Repair harm

Complete action

Acknowledge truth

Fulfill obligation


Field Method

1. Identify recurring negative pattern


2. Trace initiating thought cycle


3. Revoke one underlying mental “license”


4. Observe changes over 30 days

Emotional reactivity

Correction Strategy

---

Low noise

---

System Classification of Humans

Human development is framed as variation in capacity to hold and stabilize “light” (attention + integrity + awareness coherence).


---

Religion and Administration Model

Religion: symbolic structure for behavioral alignment

Administration: underlying causal balancing system

Outcomes are framed as delayed equilibrium responses rather than moral reward/punishment



---

Final Teaching

Attention aligned with clarity becomes stable intelligence.

When internal conflict reduces, action becomes less reactive and more precise. The system is described as moving toward coherence between perception, thought, and behavior.


---

Key Takeaways

Thought is treated as a causal mechanism that produces delayed effects in reality

Self is divided into doer, thinker, and knower

Mental discipline is built through selection, holding, compression, and focus

Emotional and behavioral imbalance is corrected through “balancing” events

Attention management is central to perceived “luck” or life outcomes

Ethics function as structural constraints, not moral abstraction

The system emphasizes coherence over control
