## Overview

Claude Mythos is Anthropic's most advanced AI system to date, positioned above prior Claude iterations in reasoning, problem-solving, and cybersecurity capability.

It was initially leaked via a CMS misconfiguration in March 2026 and later officially acknowledged on April 7, 2026. Internally codenamed **“Capybara”**, it is described as a higher-tier model beyond Claude Opus.

The system is characterized by advanced contextual reasoning, autonomous decision-making capabilities, and strong performance in high-stakes analytical domains.

## Key Features

### Advanced Reasoning
Claude Mythos demonstrates strong multi-step reasoning abilities across:
- Software engineering
- Scientific research synthesis
- Strategic planning
- Complex logical deduction

Internal benchmarking reports indicate significant performance gains over Claude Opus-class models in reasoning-heavy evaluations.

---

### Cybersecurity Capabilities
Claude Mythos is described as having advanced offensive and defensive security capabilities, including:
- High success rates in identifying exploit paths in controlled environments
- Discovery of previously unknown vulnerabilities in complex systems
- Strong performance in security auditing tasks

Its use is strictly constrained to defensive research contexts due to safety concerns.

---

### Autonomous Decision-Making
The model is designed to:
- Execute reasoning chains with reduced human intervention
- Propose and act on analytical conclusions in structured environments
- Maintain audit logs of autonomous actions

Human oversight mechanisms remain mandatory for deployment scenarios.

### Enhanced Natural Language Understanding
Claude Mythos exhibits:
- Strong handling of ambiguity and context shifts

- More reliable interpretation of implicit intent in complex prompts
