# Trivium

- Who, What, When, Where, How, Why.

- Grammar: Who, What, When, Where. Logic: How. 
Rhetoric: Why. 

- Take the Grammar of a subject filter it with Logic removing contradictions. 

- Logically assemble the filtered Grammar to arrive at Rhetoric. 

- You reverse it to break down a Rhetoric or Subject using Logic to arrive an non-contradictory Grammar.
